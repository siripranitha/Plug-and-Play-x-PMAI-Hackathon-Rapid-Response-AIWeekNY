Incoming calls

Same incident?

Within 300 m, within 15 minutes of the last call, and the same type of emergency or clearly overlapping words. A car break-in next to the fire stays separate.

How urgent?

Base score by type, plus each life threat heard on any call (trapped, unresponsive, shots, children), plus a small boost for more independent callers.

Never drop a caller

A duplicate that adds a new threat raises the score and flags the incident for a dispatcher to review. Every call stays attached and readable.
